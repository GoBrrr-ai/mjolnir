## The fused-draft-decode collision with upstream work

0.32.0 landed its own fused-draft machinery for the **trtllm-gen** decode
path: a `supports_draft_decode_metadata_update` grant
(`flashinfer_trtllm_api_decode_kernel == TRTLLM_GEN and not use_dcp`) and a
**stub** `update_draft_decode_metadata` (their path needs no per-step plan
update). Our patch's three collision points and the resolution:

1. **The `__init__` grant** — ours used to overwrite upstream's. Now
   **OR-ed**: upstream's trtllm-gen grant `or` our FI-native grant
   (`enable_cuda_graph and dcp_world_size == 1 and not
   use_trtllm_decode_attention`).
2. **The method itself** — the patch now **replaces the upstream stub body**
   with the real FI-native plan advance (previously it inserted a new
   method). The `isinstance(metadata.decode, FIDecode)` guard keeps the two
   paths disjoint: trtllm-gen metadata flows through as a no-op, exactly
   like upstream's stub.
3. **The trailing anchor** — `use_cascade_attention` is gone from the
   builder (moved/removed upstream); hunks re-anchored on the stub and the
   `class FlashInferImpl` boundary. The module-level
   `_advance_fi_decode_plan_kernel` still appends at EOF.

Also gone in 0.32.0: `v1/worker/gpu/spec_decode/autoregressive/` (the
pre-0.29.1 home of 49652's target) — the Dockerfile smoke-test `touched`
list was updated to the current patched-file set (16 entries).
