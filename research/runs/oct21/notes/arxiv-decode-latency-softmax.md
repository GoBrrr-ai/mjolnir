# arXiv research brief — latency attacks for the Mjolnir Thor decode kernel (digest)
2026-10-21; arxiv-researcher; round-1 brief (no run ids — literature scan filtered against the measured state: latency-bound, 64-B L2 request wall, ring-depth lever exhausted, merge-fusion retired).
**Verdict:** four ideas proposed (declared-reference softmax, weight-gated V fetches, CTA-pair ping-pong, cluster-resident DSMEM merge); the measurement campaign closed them all — softmax timing flat at every L (softmax-reduce-every-n.md), the request wall is geometry-locked (wide-sw128-ring-inert.md), the merge is launch-bound with a frozen j-ordered sum (arxiv-decode-round2-splits-merge.md).
**Key facts:**
- Papers read in full: 2609.33410 (FoldAttention), 2311.01282 (FlashDecoding++), 2407.08608 (FA3), 2508.18850 (ClusterFusion), 2604.23553 (ClusterFusion++); screened and rejected: 2604.00028 (data point only), 2511.22333 PAT, 2410.18038 POD-Attention, 2609.00097 FFD + sparse attention (changes results; bit-faithful emulator gate), 2505.21487 GLA/GTA, green-context/SM-partitioning papers.
- Idea 1 (declared-reference softmax): fix Z_i before scanning — FoldAttention Prop 1(iii): any Z_i within ~80 binades of the row max keeps weights in range; removes the cross-lane max reduction + rescale from the S→P chain; needs a rounding-tolerance policy decision (bit-exact-only verify kills it).
- Idea 2 (weight-gated V fetches): a 128-row tile with no live keys (s_ij ≥ Z_i − T for some row in the GQA group, ≤ 7 rows) skips its V reads — the only request-COUNT-reducing form; but a genuine approximation needing `bench accuracy` gating (FoldAttention T=16: within 1.5% of lowest-BF16 error on 7 generations).
- Idea 3 (CTA-pair ping-pong): phase-offset the 2 CTAs/SM sharing one tcgen05 issue unit/TMEM ports (FA3 §B.3: 3-stage pipelining is WORSE than 2-stage — don't deepen S/P); zero-risk variant = parity flip by `cta_rank_in_sm % 2`; softmax slack was later measured (timing flat), closing it.
- Idea 4 (cluster-resident merge): partials (A,L) over DSMEM, binary tree, O(log N); NOT the retired 0.56× fused last-CTA merge (serial); needs splits/head capped at 8 (40 CTAs = 10 splits/head > portable cap 8); killed later — the merge's j-ordered sum is the bit-faithful spec.
- Ordering recommendation: 1 → 3 → 4 compatible; 2 builds on 1 + needs the accuracy gate; 1+3 compound.
